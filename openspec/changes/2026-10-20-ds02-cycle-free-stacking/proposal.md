# Proposal: Remove the cycle counter from the stacking layer

**Change:** `2026-10-20-ds02-cycle-free-stacking`
**Status:** Implemented
**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Capability:** [`rul-prediction-ds02`](../../specs/rul-prediction-ds02/spec.md)

---

## Why

The hybrid's headline number was produced by a counter, not by an ensemble.

With `beta = 1.0` the final prediction **is** `p_meta`. Until this change the
meta-features were `[pred_lstm, pred_lgb, cyc_norm]`, and the fitted meta-learner put
essentially all of its weight on the third column:

```
ridge coef [p_lstm, p_lgb, cyc_norm] = [0.0019, 0.0124, -98.4065]
lgb_meta feature importances          = [0, 0, 720]
```

Both base models were discarded. The "stacking ensemble" was reading how many cycles
the engine had flown.

The tell was in the seed spread: the hybrid scored `5.523 ± 0.001` across seeds
42/7/123 while the BiLSTM swung `±0.67`. An ensemble does not average away its
members' variance that completely — a counter does not have any.

Removing `cyc_norm` from **every** component was already measured
(`scripts/ablate_meta_cycle.py`, 5.568 RMSE) and is the wrong experiment: it also
re-trains the base models without the clock, which costs the BiLSTM +1.094 RMSE. The
clock is a legitimate production input for the base models — a fleet does know how many
cycles an engine has flown. The fix is to remove it from the layer that was supposed
to reconcile two models, and keep it in the models themselves.

## What changes

1. The meta-features become `[pred_lstm, p_lgb]`. `cyc_norm` remains channel 19 of the
   BiLSTM sequence and feature 93 of the tabular summary.
2. `select_beta_grouped(..., use_cycle_feature=False)` — the out-of-fold beta selection
   must score the same design the meta-learner is fitted on, otherwise the selection is
   optimised for a model that no longer exists.
3. `models/ds02_manifest.json` records `meta_features: 2` in both `model_shape` and
   `inference_contract`, so the width is verifiable from the manifest alone.

## Impact

| | RMSE | MAE | R² | NASA | meta weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clock in the meta-learner (previous) | 5.523 ± 0.001 | 4.795 ± 0.001 | 0.9126 | 3435.6 ± 0.7 | ~100 % clock |
| **clock removed from the meta-learner (shipped)** | **4.301 ± 0.068** | **3.181 ± 0.021** | **0.9470** | **2315.6 ± 20.0** | p_lgb 0.79, p_lstm 0.12 |

The new spread is real rather than an artefact: the meta-learner now depends on the base
models, so it inherits their variance.

- **Base models:** unchanged. No retraining rationale is claimed for them.
- **`beta`:** unchanged at 1.00, re-selected out-of-fold on the new design
  (folds `[1.00, 1.00, 0.00]`, median 1.00).
- **Artifacts:** `ensemble.joblib`, `scaler.joblib`, `bilstm_ds02.keras`,
  `ds02_manifest.json` and the root-level copies are all regenerated by the notebook.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_artifact_contract.py` — 10/10, unchanged. The contract tests
  do not pin the meta width, which is why the manifest field was added.
